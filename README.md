# Hey, I'm Kal 👋

I build AI tools that make people's lives meaningfully better.

Most AI investment is chasing automation and replacement. I'm interested in the other thing: AI that amplifies human capability, serves people who've been underserved by technology, and solves problems that actually matter. Not as a business strategy. As an orientation.

The Silicon Valley mindset of shipping first, capturing the market, and calculating costs later has been terrible for society and for the planet. I believe AI's true value isn't in replacing us; it's in releasing us from the prisons and spirals of our own making. Done right, it's a powerful learning tool, a stimulator of reasoning, and a way to make the previously unattainable possible.

The 98% of AI use cases that haven't been built yet aren't unbuilt because they're not valuable. They're unbuilt because they're not optimized for venture returns. An immigrant trying to understand asylum eligibility. A teacher drowning in grading. A job seeker who can't afford a career coach. These are the problems I work on.

## What I'm Building

🧭 **[Saathi](https://saathi.fly.dev)**. A privacy-first AI immigration advisor. Voice conversations in 50+ languages help users understand their path to legal status (asylum, DACA, naturalization) without collecting personal data. Built in collaboration with a legal nonprofit. The open-source version enables legal aid organizations to provide multilingual intake for immigrant and LEP communities.

📝 **[Autobiographer](https://autobiographer.io)**. A free AI job coach that helps people get to their dream job. It analyzes job descriptions, researches companies and their cultures, builds a personalized gameplan for upskilling and networking, and then helps with the materials: tailored resumes, cover letters, and thorough interview prep (behavioral and applied). Not a resume mill. A hope and fit maximization machine, free because job searching is stressful enough without hitting a paywall.

🛣️ **[Road Not Taken](https://roadnottaken.io)**. A personalized travel guide generator for road trips that works worldwide. For people who'd rather take the long way.

🏎️ **[RevMatch](https://revmatch.co)**. Manual sports cars with real mechanical feel are disappearing. RevMatch keeps them on the road. A 10-car enthusiast fleet (911s, Boxsters, Caymans, Quadrifoglios) bootstrapped to ~$150K ARR, powered by an end-to-end AI operations stack that runs on 0.2 FTE.

🎸 **[The Shiny Black Holes](https://shinyblackholes.com)**. A semi-autobiographical collaboration between a human songwriter and a generative music model. Songs written across two decades, finally performed. It broke a 17-year creative block.

🎓 **[Chalk](https://chalk.my)**. A shared AI-teacher workspace designed to alleviate cognitive load and support differentiated instruction. Currently in the research phase, observing elementary school teachers and understanding their challenges before building.

## Why

I have a love-hate relationship with AI.

I think it's our species' most significant invention. When properly aligned, it makes us more thoughtful, more reflective, and better versions of ourselves. But the danger isn't what AI might do to usurp us; it's what we're likely to do to each other when armed with it.

Instead of "how do I capture more value," I start with "how do I make this easier or better for someone?" The purpose is building something useful. Profit as a side-effect, and an optional one at that.

Everything I build starts from a simple premise: the human stays in the loop. The AI sharpens judgment instead of replacing it. Consciousness, both human and AI, derives meaning from purpose.
